Missing KYC Know Your Customer — Identity Verification

KYC is the regulatory obligation for a bank to verify the identity of each customer. When a customer's KYC record is missing or outdated, the bank cannot confirm who they are — this is a major compliance risk and directly raises their score.

PEP Exposure Politically Exposed Person — AML Monitoring

A PEP is a person who holds or has held a prominent public position (politicians, senior officials, executives). Because of their influence, they carry a higher risk of corruption, bribery, or money laundering — banks must monitor them more closely under FATF guidelines.
